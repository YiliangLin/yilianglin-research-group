Our invited review on gallium-based liquid metal reactor has been published in Nanoscale! Congrats, Dr. Ming Wang!
Please check the link: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2024/nr/d3nr06566a

<!--more-->

Gallium-based liquid metals and their alloys have gained prominence in the realm of flexible and stretchable electronics. Recent advances have expanded the interest to explore the electron-rich core and interface of liquid metals to synthesize various nanomaterials, where gallium-based liquid metals serve as versatile reaction media. This paper delves into the latest developments within this burgeoning field. The discussion begins by elucidating the unique attributes of liquid metals that render them suitable as reaction media, including their high metal solubility, low standard reduction potential, self-limiting oxidation and ultra-smooth surface. It then provides a comprehensive categorized summary of utilizing these features to fabricate a variety of nanomaterials, including pure metallic materials, metal-inorganic compounds, and metal-organic composites. This paper concludes by discussing the current challenges in this field and exploring potential future directions.
