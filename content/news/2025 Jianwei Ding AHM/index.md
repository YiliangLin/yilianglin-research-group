Jianwei Ding's work with our collaborators, "Bioengineering Living Biohybrid Therapeutics for Synergistic H2S Gaseous-Photothermal Cancer Eradication," has been published in ***Advanced Healthcare Materials***. Congratulations to Jianwei and all our collaborators!

<!--more-->

The study develops living biohybrids that combine engineered *Vibrio natriegens* for hydrogen sulfide production with self-mineralized copper sulfide nanoparticles. The platform integrates gaseous therapy with photothermal treatment and demonstrates antitumor activity in a preclinical mouse model.
