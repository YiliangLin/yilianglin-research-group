Our group's **first biohybrid research paper**, "An anoxygenic photosynthesis-inspired dual biohybrid for light-enhanced chemical production," has been published in **Science Advances**! Congratulations to Ying Yang, Zipeng Zhu, and all our collaborators! We extend our sincere thanks to **Prof. Xiang Gao** and **Prof. Yuanyuan Wang** for their collaboration and support throughout this work.

<!--more-->

Inspired by anoxygenic photosynthesis, we combined extracellular conjugated polymer nanoparticles as light-harvesting antennae with intracellular CdS nanoparticles in *E. coli*. The dual biohybrid couples light capture with intracellular redox chemistry and increases malate production by **nearly 30-fold relative to unmodified cells**. This study marks a milestone for our group's biohybrid research and provides a modular design strategy for light-assisted microbial chemical production.

[Read the paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aef4504).
