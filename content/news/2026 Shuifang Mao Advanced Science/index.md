Shuifang Mao's work, "Living Hydrogels: Harnessing Microorganism-Material Synergy for Next-Generation Therapeutics," has been published in ***Advanced Science***. Congratulations to Shuifang and our collaborators!

<!--more-->

The review examines how hydrogel design can protect living microorganisms, improve their retention, and control therapeutic delivery. It discusses microorganism-material interactions and applications in gut microbiota modulation, wound healing, and cancer immunotherapy, alongside the challenges of clinical translation.
